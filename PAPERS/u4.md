**Answer:**

## 2. Long Answer Questions (Part B)

### Question 2.1: Product Risk, Project Risk, and Business Risk *(Aug 2025)*
#### Q: Elaborate Product Risk, Project Risk and Business Risk with examples.
**Answer:**
1.  **Product Risk:** Risks affecting the quality, performance, or dependability of the software artifact being developed.
    *   *Example:* Software component fails memory benchmark tests or contains un-patched security vulnerabilities.
2.  **Project Risk:** Risks affecting project schedule, staffing, or financial budget limits.
3.  **Business Risk:** Risks affecting the commercial viability or organization delivering the product (e.g., market demand collapses).

---

### Question 2.2: Factors Affecting Group Communication *(Aug 2025)*
#### Q: Discuss the factors that affect Group Communication.
**Answer:**
1.  **Group Size:** As team size ($N$) grows, communication channels increase quadratically ($N(N-1)/2$), increasing noise and delay.
2.  **Team Structure:** Hierarchical teams restrict communication to formal reports; flat self-organizing teams communicate informally.
3.  **Physical Workspace Layout:** Open-plan collaborative spaces encourage frequent spontaneous discussions compared to isolated cubicles.
4.  **Team Diversity & Culture:** Diverse skill sets and cultural backgrounds require clear inclusion policies to prevent communication friction.

---

### Question 2.3: Project Management Stages with Examples *(Sept 2023)*
#### Q: Illustrate Project Management stages with examples.
**Answer:**
1.  **Proposal Writing:** Submitting contract bids and feasibility studies to win client funding.
2.  **Project Planning & Scheduling:** Creating WBS and Gantt charts allocating developer tasks.
3.  **Project Execution & Monitoring:** Conducting daily stand-ups and tracking burn-down charts to manage schedule slippage.
4.  **Risk Management:** Assessing project risks and executing mitigation strategies.
5.  **Project Closure & Post-Mortem:** Conducting retrospective reviews and archiving project artifacts.

---

### Question 2.4: Popular Project Size Estimation Techniques *(Sept 2023)*
#### Q: List and identify popular project size estimation techniques used in Software Engineering and briefly discuss them.
**Answer:**
1.  **Source Lines of Code (SLOC / KLOC):** Counting thousands of lines of source code.
2.  **Function Point (FP) Analysis:** Quantifying user-facing inputs, outputs, queries, files, and external interfaces.
3.  **Object Points:** Counting number of UI screens, database tables, and reports (used in COCOMO-II prototyping phase).
4.  **Use Case Points (UCP):** Estimating system size based on number of Use Cases and Actor complexity.
5.  **COCOMO-II Algorithmic Modeling:** Mathematical estimation based on KSLOC converted from FPs multiplied by cost drivers.
