## 1. Short Answer Questions (Part A)

### 1.1 Identify and list the major drawback of the Spiral Model. *(Sept 2023)*
**Answer:** The major drawback of the Spiral Model is its **high financial cost and heavy dependence on risk assessment expertise**. If risk analysis is faulty or performed by inexperienced engineers, the project can easily fail or experience major budget overruns.

---

### 1.2 Differentiate between Incremental and Iterative Development. *(Aug 2025)*
**Answer:**
*   **Incremental Development:** Building and delivering the software in functional parts/chunks (increments), where each increment adds new functional features to the working system.
*   **Iterative Development:** Refining and improving the existing codebase and features through repeated cycles (iterations) of feedback and enhancement.

---

### 1.3 List three essential processes involved in system planning and designing phase of SDLC. *(Sept 2023)*
**Answer:**
1.  **Requirements Elicitation & Analysis:** Discovering domain constraints and customer expectations.
2.  **Architectural Design:** Establishing overall system component structure and communication channels.
3.  **Interface Specification:** Defining Provides and Requires APIs and user interface layouts.

---

### 1.4 List the essential factors on which software process model selection is based. *(Sept 2023)*
**Answer:**
1.  **System Size and Complexity:** Large-scale systems favor plan-driven models; small/medium systems favor agile.
2.  **Requirements Volatility:** Unstable user requirements favor incremental/agile models; stable requirements favor waterfall.
3.  **Safety and Regulatory Constraints:** Safety-critical systems require upfront formal specification models.
4.  **Team Expertise & Customer Availability:** High customer involvement favors iterative agile approaches.

---

### 1.5 Requirement Analysis does not deal with the activity diagram. Justify. *(Sept 2023)*
**Answer:** Requirement Analysis focuses strictly on **what** services and operational constraints the customer needs (problem domain). Activity diagrams, on the other hand, model **how** internal system workflows and execution control paths operate during system design (solution domain).

---

### 1.6 When is the risk analysis in the Spiral Model performed? *(Sept 2023)*
**Answer:** Risk analysis is performed **at the beginning of every spiral iteration loop (Quadrant 2)**, immediately after identifying iteration objectives and alternative solutions.

---

### 1.7 Suggest the best way to conduct requirement validation review. *(Sept 2023)*
**Answer:** The best way is to conduct a **multidisciplinary formal review meeting** (involving customers, system architects, and test engineers) using structured review checklists to systematically verify validity, consistency, completeness, realism, and testability.

## 2. Long Answer Questions (Part B)

### Question 2.1: Traditional Development vs. Agile Development *(Aug 2025)*
#### Q: Differentiate between Traditional Development and Agile Development techniques.
**Answer:**

| Dimension | Traditional (Plan-Driven) Development | Agile Development |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lifecycle Approach** | Sequential phases (Waterfall model). | Iterative sprint cycles (2–4 weeks). |
| **Requirements Management** | Complete upfront SRS sign-off before coding. | Fluid backlog prioritized continually. |
| **Customer Role** | Involved mainly during initial specs & acceptance. | Continuous daily customer collaboration. |
| **Documentation** | Heavy formal documentation deliverables. | Working code prioritized over documentation. |

### Question 2.2: Online Banking System Requirements *(Aug 2025)*
#### Q: Considering an Online Banking System, write any three Functional and Non-functional requirements.
**Answer:**
*   **Functional Requirements:**
    1.  *FR-1 (Fund Transfer):* System shall allow users to transfer funds between active accounts within 3 seconds.
    2.  *FR-2 (Transaction History):* System shall display searchable transaction history for the past 12 months.
    3.  *FR-3 (Bill Payment):* System shall allow setting up recurring automated bill payments.
*   **Non-Functional Requirements:**
    1.  *NFR-1 (Security):* System shall enforce Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) and 256-bit SSL encryption.
    2.  *NFR-2 (Availability):* System uptime shall exceed 99.99% annually.
    3.  *NFR-3 (Performance):* Login page response time shall not exceed **1.0 second** under normal network loads.

### Question 2.4: Stakeholder Requirements Conflicts in Hospitality System
#### Q: Suggest stakeholders in a Hospitality Management System and explain why stakeholder requirements conflict.
**Answer:** Guest, Receptionist, Manager, Housekeeping, Finance, System Admin. Conflicts arise due to opposing goals (e.g. quick check-in vs mandatory billing address validation).

### Question 2.5: Formal Methods Advantages & Industry Reluctance
#### Q: Suggest advantages of developing a formal specification and using it in a formal development process. Why has industry been reluctant to adopt formal methods?
**Answer:** Advantages: Unambiguous math logic, formal proof of correctness. Reluctance: High math skill barrier, heavy time investment.
